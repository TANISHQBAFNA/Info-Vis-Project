# Mumbai Exposure Index — data notes

Theme scores are **percentile ranks across 24 BMC administrative wards**, monsoon-weighted:

- Flood / drainage 35%
- Heat / housing 25%
- Air / health 20%
- Services / density 20%

Signals (not an official BMC index):

- Mumbai Climate Action Plan (MCAP) 2022 — heat (M East), flood hotspots (F North = 54)
- IISER combined flood vulnerability index, 2025 — 13 high-flood wards
- Census 2011 / OpenCity ward tables — population, area
- Typical AQI geography (Deonar, Trombay, airport, highways)
- Estimated slum share is order-of-magnitude, for the About cards

Live layer:

- Daily rainfall and current AQI from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) at ward centroids
