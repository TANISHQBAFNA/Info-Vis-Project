    01 · Establishing

    Virginia is not one housing market.

    Color is a typical home, live from Zillow, for every county and independent city. The strip below is that same list, sorted. Scroll — the camera goes in on the two ends.

    02 · Falls Church

    The ceiling is a city you can miss on the map.

    Independent city, Northern Virginia. A typical home here is the top of this commonwealth. The line is ten years of ZHVI — not a listing, the same index as the color.

    03 · Buchanan

    Drive west. Same state. Same instrument.

    Coalfields. Cheapest typical home on this map. The 10-year line is the same ZHVI series as Falls Church. If the ceiling compounded, the floor had a different decade.

    04 · Pull back

    Fifteen times apart, still one commonwealth.

    Two marks stay lit. Indexed to 100 at the start of the decade: did the gap widen or close? That is a finding the map alone cannot say.

    05 · Fairfax

    Most of the expensive story sits here.

    Not the peak, not the floor. Northern Virginia’s typical home — live ZHVI against live ZORI. Clay is the house. Sage is the rent. The rest of the Virginia act holds on this county.

    06 · The ratio

    The house is years of rent, stacked.

    Same county, new color: ZHVI ÷ (ZORI × 12). The inset walks that ratio year by year. If the number rose, buying with rent got harder even when both lines went up.

    07 · Next year

    The metro may speak for next year. Not for 2031.

    Index 10 years ago = 100. Solid is observed ZHVI. The cone is year-1 Zillow metro (Washington, DC) then a trailing CAGR band. A trend extension — not a priced 5-year model. The map stays.

    08 · BMC

    Mumbai does not publish that walk.

    No 10-year ward price. The ladder is this year’s stamp-duty floor for 24 BMC wards, tallest first. Frozen FY 2026-27 at 0% revision. Across the creek, two more Mumbais wait — without even this.

    09 · M East

    Start where the floor is low and the count is high.

    Eastern suburbs. Ready reckoner, not a listing. Rank among 24, 2011 households, and years of citywide 2BHK rent against a 1,000 ft² RR floor — city rent, ward floor. Two different geographies in one ratio.

    10 · Malabar Hill

    Same instrument. The top of 24.

    Ward D. The camera crossed the harbour inside BMC. The ready reckoner did not change method. The highlighted bar is this ward against every other floor on the island and in the suburbs.

    11 · Two homes

    Not a hectare. A thousand square feet.

    Color is the ready-reckoner value of 1,000 carpet ft². That is the BMC version of Falls Church / Buchanan — a home, not a hectare. City 2BHK rent sits under the bars, unmapped, so the years-of-rent ratio stays honest.

    12 · Harbour

    The camera crosses the creek.

    BMC is the city with a rate. Navi Mumbai is the city CIDCO already built. Mumbai 3.0 is a new town on 124 villages — a plan, not a floor. Three shapes. Three data regimes. One metropolitan bet.

    13 · Navi Mumbai

    This city was planned. It already has people.

    NMMC. CIDCO 1971, corporation 1992. Census 2011 people and households against BMC’s 2011 stock. Node-level ready reckoner exists — Vashi, Nerul, Kharghar — and is not compiled here. The missing number is the mapped floor.

    14 · Mumbai 3.0

    323 square kilometres. No stamp-duty floor yet.

    Karnala–Sai–Chirner. MMRDA notified 15 Oct 2024. Area against NMMC’s. Dashed because the envelope is schematic, not a cadastral NTDA sheet. A planning object. Near Atal Setu, the new airport, the port.

    15 · Close

    Two metros. Two instruments. Three Mumbais.

    Virginia published a 10-year walk, so the gap can move in time. Mumbai published a floor, a census, and a plan. The bars compare range: ZHVI max/min against ASR max/min. Not the same kind of inequality — and that is the point. Scroll back. The camera still flies.
