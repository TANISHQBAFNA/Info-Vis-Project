    01 · Virginia

    A house is years of rent, stacked.

    Fairfax starts the film. Typical home versus typical rent — one county, live Zillow, not a stock photo. Scroll. Map holds. Story advances.

    02 · Typical home

    What a home costs.

    Each shape is a Virginia county or independent city. Color is Zillow’s mid-tier ZHVI, fetched in the browser. Hover to spotlight. Click to lock the about panel.

    03 · Years of rent

    How many years of rent equal the house.

    ZHVI ÷ (ZORI × 12). Same file, same month, no extra model. High years: home ran ahead of rent. Low years: rent already buys a lot of house.

    04 · Asking rent

    The rent itself.

    Zillow Observed Rent Index — asking rent for the typical unit, not HUD’s 2-bedroom FMR. HUD stays an overlay. Many Northern Virginia counties share one Washington HMFA number on purpose.

    05 · Market

    How hot the listing.

    Market heat, days to pending, inventory, price cuts — live Zillow county series. Chips still work if you want a different cut. Map color tweens. It does not wipe.

    06 · Ten-year walk

    Rent × price, year by year.

    Connected scatter, not another choropleth. Diagonals are 15 / 25 / 35 years of rent to buy. Scrub the year. Play draws the path. Hold a county to ghost a second walk.

    07 · Cone

    Index 10 years ago = 100, then a cone.

    Year 1 of the home fan is Zillow’s metro forecast (Fairfax → Washington, DC — not Washington, IN). Years 2–5 and all rent years are trailing CAGR ± 1σ. Trend, not a priced model.

    08 · Cut

    Mumbai does not publish this walk.

    No official locality rent ₹/ft². No Residex JSON. No 10-year ward series. What exists: IGR ready reckoner — the stamp-duty floor — and Census 2011 stock. Film cuts. Map becomes 24 BMC wards.

    09 · Ready reckoner

    A floor, not a sale.

    ASR ₹/ft² is what Maharashtra uses for stamp. Usually below transacted price. M East is the default: cheaper floor, dense east suburbs. Click a ward. Ladder on the right ranks all 24.

    10 · Peak

    Malabar Hill is the ceiling of this map.

    Ward D is the 24-ward peak. Same ready-reckoner method, different island. Compare the 1,000 ft² floor and years of city 2BHK rent in the about card — city rent, ward floor.

    11 · A flat

    Not a hectare. A home.

    650 ft² and 1,000 ft² ready-reckoner values, a 6% stamp illustration on 650 ft², years of Magicbricks city 2BHK rent to cover 1,000 ft². Derived from ASR. Not a listing scrape.

    12 · Stock

    Who was counted in 2011.

    Households versus ready-reckoner floor. Island city in clay, suburbs in sage. Dense cheap wards sit up and left; expensive island wards sit right. Census 2011 — old, official, not weather.
